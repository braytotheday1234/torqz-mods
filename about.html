About Torqz

Made for BeamNG players.

Torqz Mods is a small BeamNG.drive modding project focused on building useful mods and showing how they come together.

Why Torqz

Useful ideas first.

I want Torqz projects to add something people can actually enjoy using in BeamNG.drive, without filling the site with fake releases or made-up stats.

What I'm Building

Torqz Garage.

Project 01 is focused on vehicle ownership, mileage, vehicle data, and future garage features that make cars feel more persistent across gameplay.
