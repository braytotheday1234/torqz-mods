About Torqz

Quality before quantity.

Torqz Mods is a BeamNG.drive modding brand focused on strong presentation, useful releases, clear documentation, and a better experience from preview to installation.

Why Torqz exists

Mods deserve a real home.

Torqz Mods is being built around one simple idea: the website around a mod should feel intentional too. That means clean release pages, real screenshots, clear compatibility information, sensible installation steps, and support links that are easy to find.

The brand is still developing, so Torqz does not claim fake history, users, downloads, reviews, or release numbers. The site is designed to grow naturally as real projects are completed.

Principles

What Torqz should stand for

01

Honest information

If a version, compatibility note, screenshot, or download does not exist yet, the site says so instead of inventing it.

02

Professional presentation

Typography, media, layout, and interactions should make every release feel deliberate without burying users in decoration.

03

Useful support

Installation instructions and issue reporting are treated as part of the release experience, not an afterthought.
